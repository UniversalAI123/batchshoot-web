Your footage, your choices

BatchShoot is provided by Universal AI LLC, a Maryland company (“we”). This notice covers the BatchShoot iPhone app and this website. Updated November 14, 2026.

The short version: your videos and their sound are edited on your iPhone and are not uploaded to us. We do not sell your information, show ads, track you across apps, or train AI on your content. An account is optional.

What stays on your iPhone

Your recordings, their sound, the transcript made from them, your edits, captions, exports and settings are stored on your device. We do not receive them. Removing the app removes its local copies, so keep your originals and finished videos somewhere safe. Anyone who uses your unlocked phone can see saved work.

Camera and microphone are used only when you record in the app. Photos access is asked for when you save a finished video, and when the app keeps a copy of a take recorded with its camera (Settings has a switch for that). Notifications, if you allow them, tell you when a batch or an export is ready. BatchShoot does not make faceprints or voiceprints and does not try to identify the people in your videos.

What we collect, and why

We collect only what the optional features need:

  • Account details (if you sign in): your email address, an account ID, and your name when Apple or Google passes it on and you allow it. Used to sign you in and keep your account.
  • Claude suggestion requests (only after you agree on that screen): the words of your take, any note you type, and up to four still pictures you pick. Used to produce the suggestion. Your video and audio files are not sent.
  • Usage records: how many Claude suggestions your account has used and what they cost us, to apply the monthly allowance and prevent abuse.
  • Plan status (when plans are on sale): whether your account has an active subscription, from Apple through RevenueCat. We never receive your card number.
  • Messages you send us: your email address and whatever you write when you contact support.
  • Connection information: like any internet service, our providers see your network address and the time of a request when the app contacts them.

We do not collect your location, contacts, advertising identifier, browsing history or health information. The app contains no advertising or analytics tracking kits, and it does not report crashes to us. This website uses no cookies and no trackers.

Who helps us run it

These companies process information for us, each only for the job named:

  • Supabase: holds your account email, sign-in session and usage records.
  • Apple and Google: confirm who you are when you choose to sign in with them.
  • Resend: delivers sign-in and deletion codes by email.
  • Anthropic: receives the text and pictures of a Claude suggestion you asked for, through our server. How it handles them is described in Anthropic’s privacy policy; we do not promise that a provider keeps nothing.
  • RevenueCat and Apple: confirm subscription status when plans are on sale.
  • Wikimedia Commons: receives the search words when you look for an illustration.
  • GitHub: hosts this website.

On iPhones that support it, Apple may download speech assets to your device; that request does not include your footage. We may also disclose information when the law requires it, to protect people’s safety or our rights, or as part of a merger or sale of the business, in which case this notice continues to apply to it.

What we do not do
  • We do not sell your personal information or share it for advertising.
  • We do not use your recordings, transcripts or pictures to train AI models.
  • We do not track you across other companies’ apps or websites.
  • We do not send marketing email unless you ask for it.
How long we keep it

Account details and usage records are kept while your account exists and are removed when you delete it. Support messages are kept for as long as we need them to help you and to keep a record of what was agreed. Our providers may keep limited records for longer where security, billing or the law requires it. Everything on your iPhone stays there until you remove it.

Your choices and rights

Delete your account: open Settings, then Account & plan, request a deletion code, enter it and type DELETE. This removes your account and its sign-in. It does not cancel an Apple subscription (do that in your iPhone’s subscription settings) and it does not remove files on your device.

See, correct or delete your information: email contact+support@universalaillc.com from the address on your account. We may ask you to confirm a code sent to that address so we know the request is yours. We aim to answer within 30 days. You will not be treated differently for asking.

Say no: every Claude suggestion asks first; if you decline, on-device editing keeps working. You can turn off notifications, Photos access, camera and microphone in iPhone Settings.

Depending on where you live, the law may give you further rights, such as appealing a decision we make about your request. Tell us and we will handle it under the law that applies to you.

Security

Information is encrypted in transit. Account records are protected by access rules so one account cannot read another’s, and the keys to our services are held on our server, never in the app. No system can be guaranteed secure; if we learn of a breach that affects you, we will tell you as the law requires. Please do not send passwords, access keys or private footage by email.

Age, location and changes

BatchShoot is for adults. You must be 18 or older to create an account or buy a plan, and the app is not directed to children under 13. If you believe a child has given us information, contact us and we will remove it.

We are based in the United States and our providers process information there. BatchShoot is currently offered in the United States only.

If we change this notice we will change the date above. If a change is significant we will tell you in the app or by email before it takes effect.
