Your footage, your choices

BatchShoot is provided by Universal AI LLC. Updated November 9, 2026.

Core editing works on your device. Optional accounts and AI use the services below. Sharing controls ask before sending your text, selected pictures or recordings.

Editing and local storage

Chosen recordings, speech transcription, saved projects, exports, preferences, comparison timers and pilot scorecards can stay on this device. Clearing website data or removing the app can remove local copies. Keep originals and finished MP4s separately. Shared-device users may see saved work.

Camera and microphone open when you choose to record. Photos permission is requested when saving. Ready alerts use notification permission. No advertising tracking is used.

Optional Claude suggestions

When you explicitly agree, BatchShoot sends the displayed request and approved source text, brief or selected still images to Anthropic through the BatchShoot backend. Your account ID and request/usage records support access control and the pilot allowance. Raw audio/video is not sent to Claude by these suggestion controls.

Choosing not to share keeps on-device editing available. Review AI suggestions before applying them; they can be wrong. Provider handling is described in Anthropic’s privacy policy. The app does not promise zero provider retention.

Optional accounts and subscriptions

Where enabled, Supabase handles your account email, verification and session. Resend delivers sign-in codes. RevenueCat verifies subscription access using an app/customer ID and subscription records. New purchases and public signup are currently disabled; existing pilot accounts may sign in on web and iPhone.

Account deletion is in Account settings where enabled and requires a fresh code. It does not cancel Apple/Stripe subscriptions or remove this device’s files. Use subscription management to cancel a plan and device storage controls to remove local work. Service providers may retain records they need for security, billing or legal obligations; contact us about a data request.

Downloads and other sharing

The iPhone app bundles a speech model; Apple may download on-device speech assets on supported systems. Web speech assets may download from Hugging Face and jsDelivr. These download requests expose ordinary connection information, not your footage.

Optional web workspace or cloud preview controls can upload chosen recordings, including their sound, to the named service after your confirmation. Editable handoffs and review files contain private material and go only where you choose to send them. Local reports and time comparisons are not automatically uploaded.

Support and choices

Contact contact+support@universalaillc.com for privacy or support requests. Reports can be previewed before you share them. Do not send passwords, API keys or private footage in an ordinary support email.

The current remote AI pilot is restricted to an approved company account. On-device editing remains available without remote AI. Contact support with questions about your account or privacy choices.

Optional crash reports

Where available in an enabled iPhone build, crash reporting is optional and off by default. If you turn on Share crash reports, Sentry receives crash times, app versions, build identifiers and machine-code addresses. Reports exclude your recordings, transcripts, account details, error text, screenshots and session replay. Sentry receives your network address in transit.

Turning reporting off stops new reports and clears the local reporting queue; a request already in transit may finish. Reports already sent remain with Sentry until removed under its retention policy. Website editing does not load this native crash-reporting SDK.
