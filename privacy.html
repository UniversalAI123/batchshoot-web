Privacy policy · Last updated November 4, 2026

Your videos stay on your iPhone

BatchShoot listens to, cuts, captions and exports your recordings on your iPhone. It has no account, no ads, no analytics and no uploads. This page describes what it keeps and the sharing actions you control.

In short

  • No account
  • No ads
  • No analytics
  • No tracking
  • No uploads
  • Nothing sold or shared

The iPhone editor processes your recordings locally. Its speech model and engine are included in the current app build, so listening does not require a remote model download.

Who we are

This policy covers the BatchShoot app for iPhone. "We" means Universal AI LLC, the publisher of BatchShoot. Questions about this policy go to contact@universalaillc.com.

What stays on your iPhone

Everything below is kept inside BatchShoot's own storage on your iPhone. We have no copy of it and can't see it.

  • Your recordings. Videos you choose from Photos or Files, or record in the app. A video you choose is read where it is; BatchShoot never changes the original.
  • Transcripts. The words BatchShoot heard, their timings and a loudness profile, kept per file so the same recording isn't listened to twice.
  • Your edits and settings. The edit choices for each recording, caption fixes, product profiles (a product name, its spelling corrections and your review reminders), your shoot-day list, and preferences such as caption style.
  • A remembered batch, only if you turn it on. With "Remember this batch on this device" on, BatchShoot keeps copies of the batch's recordings, your edit choices and the last finished file for each recording, so a closed app can pick up where it left off. Turning it off forgets them.
  • Finished videos. Each video you save is kept as an MP4 in Files › On My iPhone › BatchShoot › Exports. Short-lived working files made during an export are removed after a day.
  • The speech model. Included with the app so listening can run locally.

Like any app's data, these files can be included in your iPhone's own iCloud or computer backup. That backup is controlled by you and Apple, not by us.

What leaves your iPhone, and when

Your recordings, their sound and their transcripts never leave your iPhone unless you share them yourself.

  • Speech model included in the app

    Where Inside the installed app

    What The current iPhone build bundles its speech model and engine. Listening uses those local files and does not send your recordings, sound, transcripts or typed text to a model service.

    When No speech-model download is needed in the current bundled iPhone build.

  • Things you share yourself

    What A finished video, or a batch report you copy or save. A report lists file names, product names and timings, so check it before you paste it somewhere.

    When Only when you pick a destination in the iOS share sheet (Save Video, AirDrop, Messages, another app) or paste a copied report. The destination's own privacy policy applies.

  • Email you send us

    What Whatever you write, and your email address. The app never sends email by itself.

    Why Only to reply to you and fix the problem.

Camera, microphone, photos and files

BatchShoot asks for each of these only when you do something that needs it.

  • Camera and microphone: used only while you record a take in the app (Record here, or Take Video when choosing recordings). The take stays inside BatchShoot. It isn't saved to Photos unless you export it and choose Save Video.
  • Photos and Files: you pick recordings, or a music file, with the system picker. BatchShoot reads only what you choose.
  • Saving to Photos: The batch export screen requests add-only Photos permission when you choose Save finished videos to Photos. You can also save through the system share sheet. BatchShoot only adds the video; it doesn't read your library.

You can change these at any time in iOS Settings › Apps › BatchShoot.

What we don't do

  • No accounts or sign-in.
  • No advertising and no advertising identifiers. BatchShoot never asks to track you.
  • No analytics, usage tracking or crash-reporting tools.
  • No cloud processing. Speech is turned into text on your iPhone, and the claims check is a word list that runs on your iPhone. Nothing is sent to an AI service.
  • We don't sell, rent or share data, because we don't have any.

Apple's own services, such as App Store downloads and the crash reports you can choose to share with developers in iOS Settings, are run by Apple under Apple's privacy policy.

Deleting your data

  • One video: open Files › On My iPhone › BatchShoot › Exports and delete it there. Remove a recording from the batch in the app.
  • A remembered batch: turn off "Remember this batch on this device".
  • Everything: delete the BatchShoot app. iOS removes everything it stored: recordings, transcripts, edits, settings, the speech model and the Exports folder. Save any finished video you want to keep first.

Copies you saved to Photos, shared with other apps or kept in an earlier device backup stay where they are. We can't delete them for you, because we never had them.

Children

BatchShoot is a tool for adults who make product videos. It isn't directed to children under 13, and it collects no data from anyone.

Security

Your files are kept in BatchShoot's private storage and protected by your iPhone's own security, such as your passcode and iOS data protection. The speech model and engine are included in the installed app.

Changes to this policy

If what BatchShoot does with data changes, we'll update this page and its date before the new version reaches the App Store. If BatchShoot ever needs to collect something, the app will say so before it happens.
